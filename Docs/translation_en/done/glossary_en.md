# English localization glossary — final

- Law: **Things remember the place they served. A road is held from both ends.**
- Qualified variant: **Some things remember the place they served.**
- Signals: **Go / Stop / Raise**. Lantern: **flash**, **three flashes — alive**; bell: **strike**, **two quick strikes**, **a long ring**. The word *Go* permits either upward hook travel or downward beam travel, as shown by the particular mechanic.
- мостовая опора: **bridge pier**; причал: **pier** (context distinguishes the two); ближняя/дальняя опора: **near/far pier** when already clear, otherwise **near/far bridge pier**.
- плита: **plate**; кольцо: **ring**; скоба для плиты: **bracket**, **plate bracket**; скобы засова: **brackets**.
- стопор (каната): **rope clamp**; струбцина: **clamp** (woodworking clamp in context); клин: **wedge**; шпонка: **spline**; ограничитель верстака: **stop**.
- ворот у затвора: **winch** throughout (final agreed standard). Затвор: **sluice / sluice gate**; рычаг: **sluice lever**; рейка уровня: **water-level gauge**.
- hinges: **original hinges** (родные петли), never 'native hinges'; hinge recesses: **sockets**; threshold: **threshold**; notch: **notch**.
- ходики: **wall clock**; карманные часы: **pocket watch / watch**. Match time **minute for minute**.
- шаль/платок: **shawl**; тряпка: **cloth**; лежанка: **sleeping ledge**, compact UI **stove sleeping ledge**.
- домовой/хранитель печи Тихон: **stove-keeper / keeper of the stove**; хозяйка: **mistress** in his old household register. He is not a cat or pet; paws and whiskers reflect source anatomy.
- горница Нины: **parlour**; жилая комната в Дунином доме: **living room**; сени: **entrance hall**, max_len12 UI **Entry hall**; галерея: **gallery**.
- ЗМ / ЗГ: preserve the **Cyrillic letters** on the physical tags and explain **mill mirror / gallery mirror** in English. The final TSVs preserve these Cyrillic tags.
- Bird drawing: **bird**, a deliberate generalisation of the source «синица»; cap, boots, scarf and boat motifs unchanged.
- крестики-нолики: **noughts and crosses**; circles: **noughts**, Xs: **crosses**. Consistent British spelling (woollen, recognised).
- Mama = мама; Mother = матушка. Aunt Dunya. Agata, Nina, Ilya / Ilyusha, Ulyana / Ulya, Varvara, Mitya, Pelageya, Pavel, Tamara, Fyodor Lukich, Dunya / Evdokia, Tikhon, Aksinya, Savely.
- Olkhovka, Zarechye. The Prokhorovs' house / Aksinya Prokhorova in the letter address.
